Student Portal

What You Can Access on the Portal

  • Learning Dashboard – View your courses and academic progress.
  • Assignments and Learning Materials – Access notes, coursework, and submit assignments online.
  • Exam Results and Academic Records – Check your performance and download academic reports.
  • Class Timetables – Stay updated with your class schedules and practical sessions.
  • School Announcements – Receive important updates from the administration and lecturers.
  • Fee Statements – View your fee balance and payment history.
  • Student Communication – Connect with lecturers and receive course updates.

Why Use the Student Portal?

The portal helps students manage their studies more efficiently by providing:

  • 24/7 access to academic resources
  • Easy communication with lecturers and administration
  • Real-time updates on academic progress
  • A centralized platform for all learning activities

How to Access the Portal

  1. Click the Student Portal Login button below.
  2. Enter your school code (722) and admission number (e.g. 10672). Do not enter the full admission format (e.g. CPOTT/10672/01/26).
  3. Enter your Password.
  4. Access your dashboard and begin managing your academic activities.
